Privacy Policy for Mayko Delivery

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

Mayko Delivery respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use the Mayko Delivery application and related services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Mayko Delivery, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information, including phone number or email address.
  • Pickup and delivery addresses.
  • Package, order, merchant, or delivery details you provide.
  • Transaction information such as payment amount and payment status.
  • Information necessary to operate driver or merchant features.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as PayPal. Mayko Delivery does not directly collect or store your complete credit card or debit card number. Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and complete delivery services.
  • Connect customers, drivers, and merchants when necessary for a delivery.
  • Calculate delivery prices and process transactions.
  • Communicate about orders, deliveries, payments, or support requests.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of Mayko Delivery.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary to provide the service, including with drivers, merchants, payment processors, hosting or technology providers, or when required by law.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Mayko Delivery services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, protect the service, and comply with legal obligations.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact Mayko Delivery to ask questions about your information or request access, correction, or deletion when applicable.

8. Children's Privacy

Mayko Delivery is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

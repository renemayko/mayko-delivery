Mayko Delivery - Delete Your Account

Mayko Delivery LLC allows Customers, Drivers, and Merchants to request permanent deletion of their accounts and associated personal data.

Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open the Mayko Delivery application.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Open your account dashboard.
  4. If available, select "Delete Account".
  5. Confirm your deletion request.

Option 2: Request Account Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the application or the Delete Account button is unavailable, you can request account deletion by email.

Send your request from the email address associated with your Mayko Delivery account.

Email: contact@maykodelivery.com

Subject: Mayko Delivery Account Deletion Request

Request Account Deletion

Do not send your password, Social Security number, or identity documents by email.

What Information Will Be Deleted?

Once your request is verified and processed, your account and associated personal information will be deleted, except information we must retain for legal, financial, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution obligations.

Certain transaction and payment records may be retained when required by applicable law. We may verify account ownership before processing an email deletion request.
